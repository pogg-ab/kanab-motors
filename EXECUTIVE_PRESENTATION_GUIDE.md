## 🏛️ Pillar 1: Customer Compliance & Smart Multi-Tax Engine

### 💡 Simple Explanation
Every customer and vehicle type is evaluated dynamically against Ethiopian Revenue Authority (MoR) statutory tax rules (15% Standard VAT, 5–10% Automotive Excise Duty, and 2% Commercial Withholding Tax). Exemption claims or custom rate overrides require mandatory audit justification reasons.

### 🖥️ Live Demonstration Steps
1. **Customer Tax Status Setup:**
   * **Page:** `Customers & CRM` (icon: 👥)
   * **Button to Click:** **`+ New Customer`** (or click the Edit pencil icon on an existing customer)
   * **What to Show:**
     * Point out the **Withholding Tax (WHT) Liable** card toggle.
     * When toggled ON, the customer is designated as an authorized corporate withholding agent eligible to deduct 2% commercial tax at source.
2. **Statutory Tax Rules Hub:**
   * **Page:** `Invoices & Billing` (icon: 📄)
   * **Tab to Click:** **`Tax Rules & Rates Hub`**
   * **Button to Click:** **`+ Add Tax Rule`**
   * **What to Show:**
     * The 3-tier statutory priority evaluation sequence:
       * **Priority 1:** Excise Tax (5% for Three-Wheelers / 10% for Motorcycles).
       * **Priority 2:** Value Added Tax (15% Standard VAT).
       * **Priority 3:** Commercial WHT (2% Withheld at Source).
     * Show how tax rates adapt automatically according to vehicle category and customer tax liability.

## 🏛️ Pillar 2: Automated Invoice Settlement & Deposit Allocation

### 💡 Simple Explanation
When generating a sales invoice for a committed order, the financial engine automatically retrieves all eligible advance deposits (from proforma/booking) and applies them directly against the invoice payable. It computes the net customer balance due with zero manual math, eliminates duplicate allocations, and protects company revenues.

### 🖥️ Live Demonstration Steps
1. **Invoice Generation:**
   * **Page:** `Invoices & Billing` (icon: 📄)
   * **Button to Click:** **`+ Generate Sales Invoice`**
2. **Interactive Live Calculation:**
   * Select an approved booking (e.g. `BKG-2026-00001 — Dawit Haile`).
   * Observe the **Live Multi-Tax Settlement Engine**:
     * Vehicle Subtotal + Excise + VAT = **Gross Commercial Total**.
     * 2% Commercial WHT deducted = **Net Customer Payable**.
3. **Automated Deposit Allocation:**
   * Point out the checkbox: **`Automatically allocate available customer deposits to invoice settlement`**.
   * Show that prior customer advance payments automatically deduct from Net Customer Payable to display the true **Outstanding Customer Balance Due**.
   * If a deposit exceeds the invoice amount, highlight the **`⚠️ Excess Deposit Identified`** banner which safely reserves excess funds for customer credit or refund workflows.
4. **Submission & Settlement:**
   * Click **`Generate & Submit for Approval`**.
   * Navigate to `Approvals` (icon: 🛡️) and click **`Approve`** to mark the vehicle booking as **`SETTLED`** and transition the vehicle unit status to **`SOLD`**.

## 🏛️ Pillar 3: Non-Cash Withholding Tax (WHT) Hub & Credit Transfers

### 💡 Simple Explanation
When a corporate client withholds 2% tax at source, Finance records this as an official non-cash credit entry linked to the invoice. By statutory policy, this withholding deduction is strictly non-refundable upon order cancellation (only actual cash deposits are refundable). Furthermore, clients holding credit balances can transfer funds directly to sister firms or vehicle orders.

### 🖥️ Live Demonstration Steps

#### A. Commercial Withholding Tax (WHT) Tracking Hub
* **Page:** `Invoices & Billing` (icon: 📄)
* **Tab to Click:** **`Withholding Tax (WHT) Tracking Hub`**
* **Buttons to Click:**
  * **`Record Settlement`** (Shield icon) in the Actions column:
    * Enter Withheld Amount (ETB), physical Withholding Certificate #, and issue date.
    * Highlights the **Customer Ledger Atomic Posting Preview** posting a non-cash `WHT_SETTLEMENT` entry.
    * Click **`Post Non-Cash Settlement`**.
  * **`Update Certificate`** (Certificate icon):
    * Updates missing physical tax receipts to **`✓ Verified On File`** or **`★ Claimed Tax Credit`**.

#### B. Two-Sided Atomic Customer Credit Transfer
* **Page:** `Excess & Refunds` (icon: 💳)
* **Tab to Click:** **`Excess & Customer Credit`**
* **Button to Click:** **`Transfer Credit`** (in the Actions column of any customer holding available credit, e.g. *Abyssinia Logistics PLC*)
* **What to Show:**
  * Select the recipient customer account (e.g. *Dawit Haile*).
  * Enter Transfer Amount (e.g. `ETB 20,000`).
  * Select an optional destination vehicle order.
  * Point out the live **Two-Sided Atomic Ledger Preview** showing side-by-side debit of source credit and credit of recipient account.
  * Click **`Confirm & Post Credit Transfer`**.

## 🏛️ Pillar 4: Vehicle Delivery & Official Dual-Document Handover Pack

### 💡 Simple Explanation
Before any assembled vehicle is released from the assembly yard, both technical inspection (PDI) and financial settlement must be 100% verified. Once approved, dispatch generates two formal, legal documents formatted for official A4 print: the Security Gate Pass and the Customer Acceptance Handover Pack.

### 🖥️ Live Demonstration Steps
* **Page:** `Delivery & Handover` (icon: 🚗)
* **Button to Click:** **`Gate Pass / Release Documents`** (Eye/Printer icon on any authorized delivery order)

#### Document 1: Official Delivery Voucher & Gate Pass
* Click Tab: **`1. Delivery Voucher (Gate Pass)`**
* **What to Show:**
  * Official corporate header: *KANAB MOTORS PLC — Automotive Assembly, Commercial Distribution & Dispatch Operations*.
  * Vehicle Particulars: Chassis / VIN, Engine Number, Colour tag, Engine CC/Power, and Odometer reading.
  * Clearance Badges:
    * `✓ PDI PRE-DELIVERY INSPECTION VERIFIED (7/7)`
    * `✓ FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT CLEARED (100% PAID / AUTHORIZED)`
  * Dual Clearance Signatures: *Security Gate Clearance Officer* & *Releasing Dispatch Officer*.
  * **Button to Click:** **`Print Delivery Voucher`** — Demonstrates clean, isolated full A4 sheet print preview without background dashboard clutter.

#### Document 2: Handover Form & Declaration (Handover Pack)
* Click Tab: **`2. Handover Form & Declaration (Handover Pack)`**
* **What to Show:**
  * Official **Handover Pack Checklist** with item-by-item verification:
    1. *Ignition Keys (Original & Spare Sets)* — 2 Sets
    2. *Spare Wheel & Pneumatic Tyre (100% Tread)* — 1 Unit
    3. *Vehicle Tool Kit (Jack, Handle & Wheel Spanner)* — 1 Set
    4. *Owner's Operational & Maintenance Manual* — 1 Copy
    5. *Manufacturer Warranty Booklet & Service Schedule* — 1 Booklet
    6. *Temporary Vehicle Transit Permit / Official Plates* — 1 Set
  * **Customer Statutory Handover Declaration** acknowledging roadworthy condition and legal custody.
  * Dual Signatures: *Customer / Authorized Recipient Signature* & *Kanab Motors Delivery Officer Signature*.
  * **Button to Click:** **`Print Handover Form & Pack`** — Demonstrates clean, isolated A4 legal handover document ready for signing and archiving.
