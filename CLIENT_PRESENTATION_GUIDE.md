# KANAB Motors — Client Presentation Steps

## 1. Login
- Sign in and show the main navigation.

## 2. Executive Dashboard
- Show sales, customer deposits, outstanding balances, and vehicle overview.
- Select a reporting period.

## 3. Company & Importer Profile
- Show company details, branding, importer information, and bank details.

## 4. Users & Permissions
- Show users, roles, and permissions for creating, reviewing, and approving transactions.

## 5. Product Master Data
- Show vehicle models, item codes, categories, brands, specifications, and selling prices.

## 6. Suppliers Master
- Show supplier profiles and freight forwarders.

## 7. Import Requisitions
- Show requested products, quantities, budget, and approval status.
- Show conversion of an approved requisition to a purchase order.

## 8. Purchase Orders
- Show the supplier, order lines, quantities, prices, and order status.

## 9. Shipments & Landed Cost
- **Shipment list:** Show shipment references, search, stage filters, and expected arrival dates.
- **Create shipment:** Show selection of open purchase-order lines and shipment quantities.
- **Logistics & Booking:** Show freight quotations, selection and approval, vessel booking, ports, departure/arrival dates, containers, insurance, cargo-tracking references, and milestones.
- **Document Centre (Gate):** Show import documents and attachments. Show document verification and original-paper status in Logistics & Booking.
- **Stage Tracking & History:** Show Ordered → Shipped → Djibouti Port → Ethiopian Customs Clearance → Inland Transit → Received, with recorded history.
- **Multi-Currency Costs:** Show expense types, amounts, currencies, exchange rates, and ETB values.
- **Landed Cost Engine:** Show allocation by value, quantity, or weight, calculated line costs, and the printable cost sheet. Show the Commit button only as navigation to receiving.
- **Inventory Receiving (VIN Matching):** Show shipment-line quantities, receiving warehouse, chassis/engine identifiers, and the recorded receipt.
- **Shipment reports:** Show import costs and shipment delays. Show the variance report as an illustrative preview; its current amounts are sample figures. Show Export Report as print/save-to-PDF.

## 10. Import Pipeline & Reports
- Show import progress, shipment bottlenecks, and available cost reports using a prepared record.

## 11. Vehicle Units & Chassis
- Find a received vehicle and show its model, chassis number, engine number, warehouse, and current status.

## 12. Inventory & Warehouses
- Show Stock Balances, Stock Transfers, and Stock Adjustments.
- Show Local Assembly Intake, Vehicle State Machine, and Movement History Log.
- Open one inventory report and show the available stock for the sales demonstration.

## 13. Customers & Dealers
- Open the customer you will use throughout the sale.
- Show customer type, contact details, TIN, banking details, and supporting documents.

## 14. Sales Enquiries & Quotes
- Show the customer, vehicle model, quantity, price, VAT, and salesperson.
- Show enquiry review/approval and the pre-advance slip.
- Convert the approved enquiry to a booking.

## 15. Advance Bookings
- Find the resulting booking.
- Show the model, quantity, required advance, deposited amount, outstanding balance, and status.

## 16. BRV Receipts & Deposits
- Show a payment linked to the same customer and booking.
- Show amount, bank/reference details, supporting bank slip, confirmation, and official receipt.
- Return to Advance Bookings to show the updated payment position.

## 17. Vehicle Allotments
- Show the booking and select a matching available vehicle by chassis number.
- Show allotment approval, the formal document, and history.

## 18. Sales Invoices & VAT
- For the fully paid demonstration, show any remaining payment confirmed through BRV Receipts & Deposits.
- Show the invoice's customer, booking/vehicle reference, net amount, VAT, and gross total.
- Show invoice approval, settlement information, and supporting documents.

## 19. Deliveries & Handover
- Show the PDI Checklist Station and recorded pre-delivery inspection.
- Show the delivery order and authorized release after inspection and settlement requirements are met.
- Open the gate pass and show vehicle details, customer details, and handover/signature sections.

## 20. Customer Ledger & SOA
- Select the same customer and relevant dates.
- Show receipt and invoice entries, debits, credits, and closing balance.
- Show the statement output available on the page.

## 21. Excess & Refund Payouts
- Open a prepared customer with an eligible excess/refund balance.
- Show payout details, approval/status information, and a completed example.

## 22. Spare Parts & POS
- Show the parts catalog and warehouse stock.
- Show a sample counter sale, quantities, prices, payment details, and receipt.
- Show the related parts reports.

## 23. Approval Queue & Policies
- Show a pending request, review/approval actions, and available policy controls.

## 24. Unified Document Center
- Search for a prepared document.
- Show its linked business record and open the attachment.

## 25. Audit Trail Logs
- Show a relevant transaction action, the user, timestamp, affected record, and available change details.

## 26. Reports & Analytics Hub
- Show Sales Reports and Operational Reports.
- Show Inventory & Fleet Reports, Customer Financial Reports, and Credit Sales & Stock Aging.
- Apply relevant dates and show one report for the demonstrated transactions.

## 27. Return to Executive Dashboard
- Refresh or reapply the reporting period.
- Show the management overview to finish the presentation.
